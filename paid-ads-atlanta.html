My approach

Strategy before budget

Ads are not a magic button. They amplify what is already clear: a defined audience, a good offer and a website or page that makes the next step easy. I start with those foundations so your budget is not wasted on traffic that cannot convert.

Here is how I run a campaign:

1. Goals & tracking

We decide what counts as a result (calls, form leads, bookings or sales) and set up conversion tracking so we can measure it.

2. Audience & offer

I define your target audience and local area, then build creative and copy around a clear offer.

3. Launch & test

Campaigns go live with a few variations of creative and audience, so we can learn what works.

4. Optimize & report

I review performance regularly, adjust budgets and creative, and send reports you can understand.

Platforms

Meta, Google or TikTok: where should your budget go?

Each platform serves a different moment. Google Search reaches people actively searching for what you sell. Meta (Facebook and Instagram) is strong for visual businesses and local awareness. TikTok can reach newer audiences with short video. I recommend the platform mix based on your customers and budget, and often start with one.

Measuring

Measure what matters, not vanity numbers

Reach and clicks are useful signals, but the number that matters is the cost of a real result for your business. I set up tracking so you can see leads, calls and bookings, not just impressions.

FAQ

Paid ads questions

Do you run ads for businesses in the Atlanta area?

Yes — I'm based in Atlanta, GA and run campaigns for clients across metro Atlanta (Alpharetta, Roswell, Milton, Marietta, Kennesaw, Sandy Springs, Decatur and more) as well as all of Georgia.

Which ad platforms do you manage?

Meta (Facebook & Instagram), Google, and TikTok. I recommend the platforms that best reach your audience and goals.

How is my ad budget tracked?

You get transparent reporting on spend and performance — reach, clicks, conversions, and cost per result — with regular reports you can actually understand.

How soon will I see results from paid ads?

Paid ads can start driving traffic quickly, but meaningful, scalable results build over the first few weeks as campaigns optimize. I'll set realistic expectations in our first call.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your goals, your market and the platform. We discuss a starting budget in the first conversation and adjust based on what the data shows. The ad spend itself is paid to the platform and is separate from my management.

Should I use Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google Search is strong when people are already searching for your service. Meta is strong for visual businesses and building local awareness. Many businesses start with one and add the other once tracking is in place.

Do I need a website for paid ads?

You need somewhere for people to land, such as a website or a landing page, with a clear next step. A confusing page wastes ad budget, so I check this before launching.

What is conversion tracking and why does it matter?

Conversion tracking records the actions that matter to your business, such as form submissions, calls or bookings. Without it, you only see clicks and cannot tell which ads actually produce customers.

Can you take over my existing ad account?

Yes. I can audit your current campaigns, fix tracking and structure problems, and continue optimizing from there.
