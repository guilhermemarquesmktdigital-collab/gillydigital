Do you shoot drone video in the Atlanta area?

Yes — I'm based in Atlanta, GA and shoot across metro Atlanta (Alpharetta, Roswell, Milton, Marietta, Kennesaw, Sandy Springs, Decatur and more) as well as all of Georgia.

Do I need a license or FAA Part 107?

No — I fly a sub-249g drone, which does not require an FAA license for recreational and commercial use in the US. That means faster scheduling and simpler logistics for your project.
